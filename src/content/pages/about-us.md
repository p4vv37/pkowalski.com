<strong>About Me</strong>

My journey started as a 3D Designer, and over the years, I have specialized in creating and optimizing tools that merge technology with creativity, particularly in the fields of machine learning and technical artistry.

Currently, I work at <strong>CD PROJEKT RED</strong>, where I contribute to the development of advanced game technologies. My role allows me to combine my passion for machine learning, computer graphics, and tool development to enhance game production processes.
