<p>It's a step-by-step disassembly of BMO - Adventure time character.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It's based on "Be more" (episode 28, season 5).<br>Created in Modo, post production in NukeX.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I've also created two still images.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was just a small fast project made in spare time. I wanted to learn Modo a little bit more, learn Mesh Fusion - the character is simple, so it was a good model to do that.</p>
