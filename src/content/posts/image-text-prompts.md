<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Introduction</strong></h2>

This time I'm exploring  3D object generation from a single image. For consistency, I will use an image of this armchair, that looks similar to some results of text-to-3D generations from the previous post:</p>

<p><a href="https://github.com/threestudio-project/threestudio?tab=readme-ov-file#stable-zero123" title="">Stable-zero123</a> - Method based on new Stability AI network: Stable-zero-123. <h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Summary</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Based on this simple experiment, Magic3D is, again, a clear winner here. One important difference from text-to-3d models is the lack of the Janus effect. None of the models had that issue</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It is interesting, that the results generated by the commercial solution - meshy.ai - look very similar to the results of Magic3D method.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One, possible reason why this method detected shape so well, while all others failed might be, that textual Inversion is not supported for it, so I had to provide a text prompt with a description of the object. Possibly this is why it was able to generate such a nice shape for the chair - it just knew, that it was generating a chair :) I'll try to verify this.</p>
