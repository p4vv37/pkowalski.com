I was curious how my newborn daughter, Klara, experiences the world. So I vibe-coded this simulator. Try the demo image or turn on your camera, then move the age slider to see what changes. In birth date mode, the default is Klara's birthday.

<BabyVisionSimulator />

## What this shows

I used looking tests to estimate how much detail infants can resolve. The slider interpolates between published age points. The studies did not measure vision for every week. Newborns can distinguish some colors under test conditions, so the first setting is not black and white.

The blur, contrast, and color filters are my interpretation of those results, not a recording of what Klara sees. The experimental focus filter uses estimated relative depth and assumes scene distances. It does not measure them. Camera frames are processed on your device. The demo image was generated with Gemini.
